# Foundation & Deep Excavation Monitoring

## Objective

Verify design assumptions about load-settlement behavior, validate deep foundation capacity, and protect adjacent structures during deep excavation (heave, lateral movement).

## Installation Guidelines (from Dunnicliff Ch 9, 12, 13, 17, 19, 24, 25)

### Pile Load Test Instrumentation
- **Load cell**: At pile head, calibrated
- **Telltales**: Multiple at different depths
- **Strain gages**: Along pile length (sister bar method)
- **Reference benchmarks**: Stable, outside influence zone

### Drilled Shaft Instrumentation
- **Base load cell**: At shaft tip
- **Side shear**: Strain gages on cage
- **Crosshole sonic logging**: Integrity testing
- **Tell-tales**: For load transfer distribution

### Braced Excavation Monitoring
- **Strut loads**: Load cells at each strut level
- **Wall deflection**: Inclinometers behind wall
- **Groundwater**: Piezometers inside/outside
- **Surface settlement**: Settlement plates, AMTS
- **Adjacent buildings**: Tiltmeters, settlement plates, AMTS

### Dewatering Monitoring
- **Piezometer arrays**: Inside and outside excavation
- **Flow measurement**: Sump pumping rates
- **Drawdown monitoring**: Observation wells

## Data Interpretation Guidelines (from Dunnicliff Ch 18, 19, 24, 25)

| Parameter | Normal Range | Alert Level | Critical Level | Reference |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|----------------|-----------|
| Pile head settlement (load test) | < 5% pile dia | > 10% pile dia | > 20% pile dia | Ch 24 |
| Strut load variation | ± 10% design | > 20% design | > 30% design | Ch 19 |
| Excavation wall deflection | < H/500 | > H/300 | > H/200 | Ch 19 |
| Groundwater drawdown | Design value | 2× design | Loss of support | Ch 19 |
| Adjacent building tilt | < 1:1000 | 1:500 | 1:250 | Ch 19 |
| Building settlement | < 25 mm | > 50 mm | > 75 mm | Ch 19 |
