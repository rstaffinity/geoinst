# Instrument Categories

Geotechnical instruments are grouped by **what they measure**. The four foundational categories below cover the vast majority of field-monitoring programs.

---

## 1. Lateral Movement

**Instruments**: Inclinometers, Tiltmeters, In-place Inclinometers (IPIs)

**Applications**: Slope stability, tunnel linings, retaining walls, landslide early-warning.

**Principle**: A probe or fixed array measures deviation from vertical along a guide casing installed in boreholes. Repeated surveys (or continuous IPI readings) reveal shearing planes and the depth of movement.

**Typical readout**: deviation-vs-depth profiles; cumulative displacement plots.

**RST products**: Digital MEMS inclinometers, RSTAR wireless tilt arrays. See [RST Inclinometers](../rst-instruments/inclinometers.md).

---

## 2. Subsurface Water

**Instruments**: Piezometers (vibrating wire, standalone, multi-level)

**Applications**: Pore water pressure monitoring, dam seepage detection, dewatering for excavations, groundwater studies.

**Principle**: A diaphragm and pressure transducer measure water pressure at a sealed filter tip. Vibrating-wire sensors convert pressure to a frequency signal that is robust over long cable runs.

**Typical readout**: hydraulic head (m) vs time; pressure (kPa).

**RST products**: Vibrating wire piezometers, RSTAR wireless piezometer nodes. See [RST Piezometers](../rst-instruments/piezometers.md).

---

## 3. Deformation / Settlement

**Instruments**: Extensometers (rod, magnetic, multipoint), Settlement plates, Hydrostatic profile gauges

**Applications**: Embankments, soft-soil consolidation, deep excavations, pile load tests.

**Principle**: Anchor points at multiple depths are referenced to a surface datum. Relative movement between anchor and surface indicates compression/extension of the intervening soil/rock column.

**Typical readout**: settlement (mm) vs depth; ground-strain distribution.

**RST products**: Borehole extensometers, magnetic extensometer spider systems. See [RST Extensometers](../rst-instruments/extensometers.md).

---

## 4. Load & Stress

**Instruments**: Load cells, Strain gauges, Stressmeters, Rock-bolt load cells

**Applications**: Foundation load tests, rock bolt stresses, bridge structural testing, tiebacks.

**Principle**: Direct measurement of force or strain via bonded resistance foil gauges, vibrating-wire load cells, or hydraulic flat jacks. Output converted to engineering units (kN, MPa).

**Typical readout**: load (kN) vs time; creep curves.

**RST products**: Vibrating-wire load cells and strain gauges for structural monitoring.
