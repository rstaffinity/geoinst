# Durham Geo Slope Indicator (DGSI)

Durham Geo Slope Indicator is a major industry supplier of:

- [Digitilt inclinometer systems](https://durhamgeo.com/) — the long-time US standard for portable slope surveys
- V-Logger data loggers for vibrating-wire sensors
- DigiPro2 processing software for analyzing structural tilt ([DigiPro2 info](https://geneq.com/materials-testing/en/geotechnical-geotechnical-instrumentation-612/))

Cited from the FHWA manual and Dunnicliff as a primary US supplier.
