# Dunnicliff — Geotechnical Instrumentation for Monitoring Field Performance

**Author**: John Dunnicliff  
**Edition**: The definitive reference textbook on geotechnical monitoring, used worldwide as the primary standard for planning monitoring programs.

## Why This Manual Matters

Dunnicliff is the **definitive guide** for engineers designing instrumentation programs for slopes, embankments, tunnels, dams, and foundations. The book covers:

- **Planning methodology** — what to measure, where, when, and why
- **Installation guidelines** — detailed procedures for each instrument family
- **Data interpretation & presentation** — turning raw readings into engineering decisions
- **Field checklists & quality control** — practical tools for reliable monitoring
- **Contractual & procurement guidance** — specs, contracts, calibration, maintenance
