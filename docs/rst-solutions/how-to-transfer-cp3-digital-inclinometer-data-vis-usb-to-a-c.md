# How to transfer CP3 Digital Inclinometer Data vis USB to a computer

Connect the CP3 to the computer via USB cable and select ‘Transfer Files’ mode. Start the Digital Inclinalysis and select Import/Export from the diagonal arrow icon at the bottom of the screen. Choose specific sites and boreholes or All sites and All boreholes to transfer all the data.

Connect to AGM X2, then click on the Internal shared storage.

Click the ‘Android’ folder, then click on the ‘data’ folder and then click on ‘com.rstinstruments’. Click on ‘Files’

Click on Export, the zipped Digital Inclinometer data folder will show up.

Click on the Zipped Digital inclinometer folder to unzip

The Sites folders can be opened and all boreholes will be in the folders.

Open the specific borehole folder and copy the borehole profiles to the Inclinalysis directory.
